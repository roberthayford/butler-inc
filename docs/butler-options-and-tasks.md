# Butlers Inc — Butler Options & Task Taxonomy

## Virtual Butler

- Flight bookings
- Hotel bookings
- Appointment bookings
- Research and planning (meals, exercises, etc.)
- Gifts
- Other

---

## Base Butler

- House waiting (for technicians, installation, during renovations)
- Vacant property management and maintenance
- Key holding, mail sorting, delivery acceptance, plant watering
- Pick ups and drop offs, forgotten items, etc.
- Wait staff during dinner parties
- Grocery delivery and set up
- Other

---

## Baby Butler

- School runs
- School play and recital recording
- Baby sitting
- Forgotten items runs to school
- Parent respite
- Kids activity planning
- Welfare checks on elderly
- Other

---

## Bougie Butler

- Sourcing rare or high value items from other locations
- Personalised gift packages
- Special order involving travel
- Anything involving luxury lifestyle
- Luxury holiday planning
- Luxury event organising
- Other

---

## Busy Butler

- Courier and package services
- Household errands, maintenance, decoration, organisation
- Personal, confidential or sensitive errands
- Stand in, proxy attendance (e.g. house hunting, meetings, kids recital, etc.)
- Cross country errands
- Procurement services
- Corporate and personal gifting
- Other

---

## Bespoke Butler

Please describe the bespoke task you would like your Butler to carry out. This could be anything from planning a dinner party menu, sourcing vendors for a wedding, organising a birthday party, etc.

> A member of the team will call you for more information.

---

## Budget Butler

Applicable options from all of the above, offered for **non-emergency situations** where flexible scheduling allows for reduced rates.
